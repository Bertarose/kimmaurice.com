Sans étiquette · Topless at the spa

In the news

What happens when access rules turn into conflicts, and what the law says. None of these cases involves a spa in the investigation.

In Quebec

  • Quebec’s CharterSince 2016, Quebec’s Charter of human rights and freedoms has prohibited discrimination based on gender identity or expression. The CDPDJ, Quebec’s human rights commission, gives the example of a trans woman whom a gym manager barred from the women’s change room: the manager should have worked with her to find a way of using the change rooms that respected her gender identity. CDPDJ (opens in a new tab) · CDPDJ brief on gender identity, 2024 (PDF, French) (opens in a new tab)
  • Topless in publicIn Quebec, being topless in public is legal, whatever your sex or gender. According to Éducaloi, cities and pools cannot require only women to wear a top. Spas are private businesses: the question remains to be put to lawyers. Éducaloi (French) (opens in a new tab)
  • Shawinigan, 2019Two young trans women say they were refused access to the changing room and toilets of the Frank-Gauthier park pool because of their gender identity. Le Soleil (French) (opens in a new tab)
  • Montreal, Quintal poolA good example: the pool sets aside time slots for trans people and their loved ones. During these slots, the changing rooms become all-gender. City of Montreal (French) (opens in a new tab)

Elsewhere in Canada

  • Toronto, 2017A women-only spa, Body Blitz, turns away a trans woman, citing a policy forbidding “male genitalia.” CBC (opens in a new tab)

In the United States

  • San Francisco, March 2025The Russian bathhouse Archimedes Banya announces that its women’s and men’s nights will be reserved for cisgender people. San Francisco’s Human Rights Commission reviews the policy. A few days later, the bathhouse reverses course and admits trans people to these nights. KALW (opens in a new tab)
  • San Francisco, March 2025At Imperial Day Spa, an employee tells a trans man that the spa separates its guests by genitals. After a video and a protest, the spa announces that everyone may use the section that matches their gender identity. KALW (opens in a new tab)
